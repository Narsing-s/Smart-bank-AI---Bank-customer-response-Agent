# Approved demo banking guidance

These short guidance entries are illustrative, general-purpose help only. They are not bank-specific policies, financial advice, or account data. The assistant must direct users to their own bank's verified channels for urgent, private, or account-specific matters.

## UPI payment safety
Before making a UPI payment, verify the recipient name and amount. Never enter a UPI PIN to receive money; a PIN authorizes a debit. Do not approve collect requests you do not understand. Never share a PIN, OTP, or screen-sharing access with someone who contacts you unexpectedly. If you suspect fraud, contact your bank promptly using its official app or verified support channel.

## ATM cash not dispensed
If an ATM does not dispense cash but your account appears debited, keep the ATM location, date, approximate time, amount, and transaction reference. Check the status in your bank's official app and raise a complaint through its verified channel. Reversal timelines depend on the bank, transaction type, and applicable rules; do not rely on an unofficial promise.

## Suspicious banking message
Do not open unexpected links, install apps at a caller's request, or share OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVVs, or full card details. Contact the bank using the number in its official app or on the card, not a number supplied in an unsolicited message. If credentials were exposed, ask the bank to secure the affected account or card.

## Pending online transfer
Check the transfer status and reference number in the official banking app. Avoid making repeated payments until you understand the original status. If funds were debited and the recipient was not credited, use the bank's official complaint process and provide the reference number there. This demo cannot inspect real transactions.

## Update registered contact details
Use the bank's official app, website, branch, or verified process to update a registered phone number or email address. Identity verification may be required. Do not share one-time passwords in chat or with callers claiming they can update your details for you.

## Account access and balance
This assistant cannot access a customer's real balance, account, card, loan, or transaction history. Sign in directly through the bank's official app or website. Never send account credentials or full account/card numbers to a chatbot.
